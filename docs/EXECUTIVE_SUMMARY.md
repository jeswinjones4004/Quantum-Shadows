# Executive Summary

## Quantum Shadows: Securing the Post-Quantum World

### The Threat
By 2030, the arrival of fault-tolerant quantum computers will shatter the cryptographic foundations of the modern internet. Algorithms like RSA and ECC, which protect everything from bank transactions to national secrets, will be rendered obsolete. This event, known as "Q-Day," jeopardizes over $10 trillion in global digital assets. The threat is not just theoretical; "Harvest Now, Decrypt Later" (HNDL) attacks are already underway, where bad actors collect encrypted traffic today to decrypt it once quantum capability is achieved. The recent Google Sycamore milestones confirm that we are accelerating towards this tipping point.

### The Solution
**Quantum Shadows** is an open-source, "no-code-ready" platform designed to democratize access to Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC). We provide a bridge for developers, startups, and governments to migrate from legacy encryption to NIST-approved quantum-safe standards (like CRYSTALS-Kyber and Dilithium) without needing deep cryptographic expertise.

### How It Works
Our platform offers a **Quantum Resilience Toolkit**:
1.  **AI-Driven Vulnerability Scanner**: Detects weak, non-quantum-safe encryption patterns in codebases and APIs.
2.  **Migration Assistant**: Automates the transition to PQC libraries.
3.  **Zero-Code Dashboard**: allowing users to manage keys and security policies visually.

### Mission
To secure critical infrastructure, especially in vulnerable developing economies like India, before the quantum deadline. We aim to be the standard-bearer for the post-quantum migration.

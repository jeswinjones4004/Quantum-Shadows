# Problem Statement & Stakes

## The Cryptographic Crisis

### The Urgency of Q-Day
Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) is the trust anchor of the digital world. It relies on the mathematical difficulty of factoring large numbers (RSA) or solving discrete logarithms (ECC). Quantum computers, running Shor's algorithm, will solve these problems exponentially faster than classical computers. The moment a sufficiently powerful quantum computer comes online ("Q-Day"), existing digital trust collapses.

### Key Stats & Projections
*   **$10 Trillion Exposure**: The estimated value of digital assets (financial data, intellectual property, personal identity data) at risk of immediate exposure.
*   **Cybercrime Surge**: Post-quantum decryption could fuel a new era of cybercrime, potentially doubling the $8 trillion annual cost of cybercrime by 2030.
*   **IoT Vulnerability**: Billions of IoT devices with hardcoded, legacy keys will become permanently compromised botnets.
*   **Blockchain Risk**: Cryptocurrencies relying on ECDSA (like Bitcoin and Ethereum legacy wallets) could see funds drained if public keys are exposed.

### Geopolitical & Uneven Impact
While superpowers race for quantum supremacy, developing nations are often left behind in defense capabilities.
*   **India's Vulnerability**: As a rapidly digitizing nation with massive DPI (Digital Public Infrastructure) like UPI and Aadhaar, India faces unique risks. A breach in these systems could cripple the economy and compromise the privacy of over a billion citizens.
*   **Harvest Now, Decrypt Later**: Nation-state actors are currently storing massive amounts of encrypted data from rival nations. We are already in a silent cyberwar where the spoils will be claimed on Q-Day.

### The Gap
Current PQC solutions are complex, mathematical, and hard to implement for the average developer. There is a critical lack of strict tooling to make the migration easy, automated, and verifiable. **Quantum Shadows fills this gap.**
